3. Loan Purpose

The purpose of this loan is to provide funds to the Borrower, Island Tech Inc., for the expansion into new markets. The Borrower will use the loan proceeds solely for the intended purpose and will not use the funds for any other purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender.

4. Interest and Fees

   a. Interest Rate
      The interest rate for this loan shall be 3% per annum, calculated on the daily balance of the outstanding principal amount.

   b. Fees
      The Borrower shall pay all fees and expenses incurred by the Lender in connection with this loan, including but not limited to, legal fees, document preparation fees, and any other fees as may be agreed upon by the parties in writing.

   c. Late Payment Charges
      If the Borrower fails to make any payment when due, the Borrower shall pay a late payment charge equal to 5% of the amount due, or the maximum rate allowed by law, whichever is less. The late payment charge shall accrue daily on any overdue amount until paid in full.

6. Security
a. Collateral
   As security for the loan, Borrower grants Lender a first priority lien on all of Borrower's assets, including but not limited to, accounts receivable, inventory, equipment, intellectual property, and real property located in Vanuatu, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

b. Additional Security
   Borrower agrees to execute such additional documents and provide such additional security as Lender may reasonably request to secure the loan.

c. Right to Sell
   In the event of default, Lender shall have the right to sell, assign, or otherwise dispose of any collateral to satisfy the outstanding balance of the loan, without being obligated to account to Borrower for any proceeds.

d. Priority of Liens
   If any of the collateral is subject to other liens, Borrower shall ensure that the lien granted to Lender under this Agreement shall have priority over such other liens.

e. Perfection of Security
   Lender shall perfect its security interest in the collateral by filing a financing statement in accordance with the Personal Property Security Act of Vanuatu.

f. Change of Collateral
   Borrower shall promptly notify Lender of any changes in the collateral or any other material changes that may affect the value or nature of the collateral.

8. Confidentiality

The Parties agree to keep confidential all non-public information relating to the terms of this Agreement, the negotiations leading to this Agreement, and the financial affairs of the other Party, except as may be required by law or court order. Each Party shall use reasonable efforts to prevent the unauthorized disclosure of such information. This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Vanuatu. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Vanuatu.

10. Dispute Resolution

In the event of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, the Parties shall first attempt to resolve such dispute through good faith negotiations. If the Parties are unable to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days of the commencement of such negotiations, either Party may submit the dispute to mediation.

The mediation shall be conducted by a mediator mutually agreed upon by the Parties or, if the Parties cannot agree upon a mediator, a mediator appointed by the Vanuatu International Arbitration Centre. The mediation shall be conducted in accordance with the Vanuatu International Arbitration Centre Mediation Rules.

If the dispute is not resolved through mediation within thirty (30) days of the commencement of such mediation, either Party may submit the dispute to binding arbitration. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator appointed by the Vanuatu International Arbitration Centre. The arbitration shall be conducted in accordance with the Vanuatu International Arbitration Centre Arbitration Rules.

The decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding upon the Parties and shall be enforceable in the courts of Vanuatu. The costs of the arbitration shall be borne equally by the Parties unless otherwise determined by the arbitrator.

Each Party shall use reasonable efforts to cooperate with the other Party, the mediator, and the arbitrator in the resolution of any dispute. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the mediation and arbitration processes described herein are confidential and shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable rules and procedures. This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement.
