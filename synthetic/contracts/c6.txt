1. Introduction

The Lender and the Borrower hereby agree to enter into this Loan Agreement, effective as of January 16, 1973, with the following terms and conditions:

1.1. The Lender, The First National Bank of Barbaraville, is willing to lend to the Borrower, The Kimstad Manufacturing Company, the sum of $10,000,000 for the purpose of funding the Borrower's expansion project.

1.2. The Borrower shall repay the loan in accordance with the terms set forth herein, at an interest rate of 9% per annum, over a 30-year term with quarterly repayments.

1.3. This Loan Agreement is governed by the laws of Svalbard & Jan Mayen Islands.

1.4. The Parties acknowledge that this Loan Agreement constitutes the entire understanding and agreement between them regarding the subject matter hereof, and supersedes any prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements between them.

1.5. The Parties further agree that this Loan Agreement may be amended or modified only in writing signed by both Parties.

1.6. The Parties have executed this Loan Agreement as of the date first written above.

2. Parties

The First National Bank of Barbaraville, a bank duly incorporated under the laws of the State of California, with its principal place of business at 5902 Phillips Pines, Port Barbaraville, CA 33408, is hereinafter referred to as the "Lender."

The Kimstad Manufacturing Company, a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the State of Rhode Island, with its principal place of business at 393 Simon Well Apt. 155, Kimstad, RI 06008, is hereinafter referred to as the "Borrower."

3. Definitions

- "Loan Amount" shall mean the principal amount of the loan extended by the Lender to the Borrower under this Agreement, in the amount of Twenty Million United States Dollars ($20,000,000).
- "Interest Rate" shall mean the annual interest rate of 9% on the Loan Amount, as provided in Section 4.
- "Repayment Term" shall mean the thirty (30) year term of this Agreement, with quarterly repayments.
- "Expansion Project" shall mean the project described in the Loan Application submitted by the Borrower to the Lender on the Agreement Date, for the purpose of funding the Borrower's expansion.
- "Lender's Counsel" shall mean the law firm of Jones & Jones, located at 123 Main Street, Barbaraville, CA 33408, or such other counsel as the Lender may designate in writing to the Borrower.
- "Borrower's Counsel" shall mean the law firm of Carpenter & Carpenter, located at 456 Elm Street, Kimstad, RI 06008, or such other counsel as the Borrower may designate in writing to the Lender.
- "Default" shall mean any event of default as defined in Section 9.
- "Collateral" shall mean the assets pledged by the Borrower as security for the Loan Amount, as described in Section 8.
- "Agreement Date" shall mean January 16, 1973.
- "Notices" shall mean any written notices provided by either party to the other in accordance with Section 12.
- "Governing Law" shall mean the laws of Svalbard & Jan Mayen Islands, as set forth in Section 11.

4. Loan Terms

The Lender shall provide a loan to the Borrower in the amount of Two Million Five Hundred Thousand US Dollars (USD 2,500,000) (the "Loan Amount") for the purpose of funding the Borrower's expansion project. The Loan Amount shall be disbursed in a single installment on the Agreement Date.

The Borrower shall repay the Loan Amount, together with any accrued interest, in thirty (30) years, with quarterly repayments commencing on April 1, 1973. The interest rate on the Loan Amount shall be nine percent (9%) per annum, compounded quarterly.

The Loan Amount shall be secured by the Collateral, as described in Section 8. The Borrower shall not, without the prior written consent of the Lender, create, incur, assume, or suffer to exist any lien, encumbrance, or security interest upon or with respect to any of the Collateral.

The Borrower shall not, without the prior written consent of the Lender, sell, transfer, pledge, or otherwise dispose of, or create any lien, encumbrance, or security interest upon or with respect to, any of the Collateral. Any attempt by the Borrower to do so shall constitute an event of default under this Agreement.

The Borrower shall keep the Collateral insured to the satisfaction of the Lender, at the Borrower's expense, against all risks of loss or damage, and shall promptly notify the Lender of any loss or damage to the Collateral.

The Borrower shall pay all costs and expenses incurred by the Lender in connection with the Loan, including, without limitation, reasonable attorneys' fees and expenses.

The Loan Amount may not be prepaid or repaid in whole or in part without the prior written consent of the Lender, except as provided in Section 10. Any prepayment of the Loan Amount shall be applied first to any accrued interest, and then to the principal amount of the Loan.

The Loan Amount may be increased or decreased from time to time by mutual agreement of the Parties in writing. Any increase or decrease in the Loan Amount shall be evidenced by a written amendment to this Agreement, signed by both Parties.

The Loan Amount may be advanced by the Lender to the Borrower from time to time, at the Lender's discretion, provided that any such advance shall be subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

The Borrower shall promptly deliver to the Lender such financial statements, reports, and other information as the Lender may reasonably request from time to time.

The Borrower shall not, without the prior written consent of the Lender, incur any indebtedness in excess of Five Million US Dollars (USD 5,000,000).

The Borrower shall not, without the prior written consent of the Lender, enter into any material contract or agreement with any third party, except in the ordinary course of business.

The Borrower shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations in connection with the Loan and the Collateral.

5. Representations and Warranties

The Borrower represents and warrants to the Lender as follows:

5.1. Authority. The Borrower has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder.

5.2. Loan Purpose. The proceeds of the Loan will be used solely for the Expansion Project.

5.3. No Conflicts. There are no legal or other impediments to the Borrower's execution and delivery of this Agreement or to the performance of its obligations hereunder.

5.4. Financial Statements. The financial statements submitted to the Lender in connection with this Loan are true and accurate in all material respects.

The Lender represents and warrants to the Borrower as follows:

5.5. Authority. The Lender has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder.

5.6. Loan Capacity. The Lender has the financial capacity to make the Loan and to perform its obligations hereunder.

5.7. No Conflicts. There are no legal or other impediments to the Lender's execution and delivery of this Agreement or to the performance of its obligations hereunder.

The Borrower and the Lender mutually represent and warrant that they have read this Agreement, understand its terms, and are entering into it voluntarily and without duress.

6. Covenants

6.1. Loan Use. The Borrower shall use the Loan proceeds solely for the Expansion Project and shall not divert or apply the Loan proceeds for any other purpose.

6.2. Payments. The Borrower shall make the Loan payments in accordance with the repayment terms set forth in Section 4, and shall promptly notify the Lender of any changes in its financial condition or ability to make such payments.

6.3. Financial Statements. The Borrower shall provide the Lender with accurate and complete financial statements and other information as required by the Lender from time to time, and shall maintain accurate and complete financial records in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.

6.4. Compliance with Laws. The Borrower shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations in connection with the Loan and the Expansion Project.

6.5. No Liens. The Borrower shall not create any liens or encumbrances on any of its assets that would affect the Lender's security interest in the collateral.

6.6. No Default. The Borrower shall not default under any of its other obligations or agreements, and shall promptly notify the Lender of any such default.

6.7. No Bankruptcy. The Borrower shall not file for bankruptcy or insolvency, or take any action that would result in the appointment of a trustee, receiver, or similar agent for the Borrower or its assets.

6.8. Cooperation. The Borrower shall cooperate with the Lender in connection with the Loan and the Expansion Project, and shall take such actions as the Lender may reasonably request to protect the Lender's interests.

6.9. No Assignment. The Borrower shall not assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the Lender.

6.10. Notices. The Borrower shall promptly notify the Lender of any changes in its contact information.

6.11. Indemnification. The Borrower shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Lender from and against any and all losses, damages, liabilities, claims, actions, judgments, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or resulting from any breach of its covenants under this Agreement.

6.12. Survival. The covenants contained in this Section shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

7. Interest and Fees

7.1 Interest. The Borrower shall pay interest on the outstanding principal balance of the Loan at the rate of nine percent (9%) per annum, compounded quarterly.

7.2 Fees. The Borrower shall pay all fees and expenses incurred by the Lender in connection with the Loan, including, but not limited to, legal fees, document preparation fees, and administrative fees. Such fees shall be added to the outstanding principal balance of the Loan.

7.3 Payment. The Borrower shall make quarterly payments, which shall include principal and interest, on the first day of January, April, July, and October of each year, commencing on January 1, 1973.

7.4 Late Payments. If any payment is not made when due, the Borrower shall pay interest on the overdue amount at the rate of 18% per annum, compounded monthly, until the overdue amount is paid in full.

7.5 Prepayment. The Borrower may prepay the Loan without penalty at any time. However, if the Borrower prepays the Loan within the first three (3) years, the Borrower shall pay all unamortized fees and expenses incurred by the Lender in connection with the Loan.

8. Security

8.1 Collateral. As security for the Loan, the Borrower grants to the Lender a security interest in all of the Borrower's present and future assets, including but not limited to, real estate, equipment, inventory, accounts receivable, and intangible property.

8.2 Filing. The Borrower shall promptly execute and deliver to the Lender all documents and take all actions necessary or desirable to perfect, protect, and maintain the security interest granted under this Agreement, including filing financing statements as required by applicable law.

8.3 Prior Liens. The Borrower represents and warrants that, except for any liens created by this Agreement, there are no liens, encumbrances, or security interests on the collateral.

8.4 Right to Sell. The Lender shall have the right to sell, assign, or transfer the collateral, with or without notice to the Borrower, in the event of a default under this Agreement. The proceeds from such sale shall be applied to the payment of the outstanding Loan balance, including accrued interest, fees, and expenses.

8.5 Remedies. The rights and remedies provided to the Lender under this section shall be in addition to any other rights and remedies available to the Lender under applicable law, including the right to foreclose on the collateral.

8.6 Governing Law. The validity, construction, and enforcement of the security interest granted under this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Svalbard & Jan Mayen Islands.

9. Default

9.1 Events of Default. The following events shall constitute an event of default under this Agreement:

(a) failure by the Borrower to make any payment when due under the terms of this Agreement;

(b) the Borrower's bankruptcy, insolvency, or assignment for the benefit of creditors;

(c) the Borrower's failure to comply with any of the covenants set forth in Section 6;

(d) any material breach by the Borrower of any representation or warranty made in Section 5;

(e) the Borrower's failure to maintain adequate insurance as required by Section 8.

9.2 Remedies. Upon the occurrence of an event of default, the Lender shall have the right to declare all amounts owing under this Agreement immediately due and payable, and to exercise any of its rights and remedies under applicable law, including the right to foreclose on the collateral.

9.3 Acceleration. The Lender's election to declare all amounts owing under this Agreement immediately due and payable shall not constitute a waiver of any other rights or remedies available to the Lender under this Agreement or applicable law.

9.4 Notice. The Lender shall provide the Borrower with written notice of any event of default and the Lender's intention to exercise its rights and remedies under this section. The notice shall be delivered to the Borrower's address as set forth in Section 12.

9.5 Cure. The Borrower shall have thirty (30) days from receipt of the notice to cure the event of default. If the Borrower fails to cure the event of default within the thirty (30) day period, the Lender may exercise its rights and remedies under this section.

9.6 Waiver. The Lender's failure to exercise or delay in exercising any right or remedy under this section shall not constitute a waiver of such right or remedy.

10. Termination

10.1 Mutual Agreement. This Agreement may be terminated at any time by mutual written agreement of the Parties.

10.2 Event of Default. Upon the occurrence of an event of default, the Lender may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the Borrower.

10.3 Acceleration. Upon termination of this Agreement, all amounts owed by the Borrower to the Lender, including principal, interest, fees, and expenses, shall become immediately due and payable.

10.4 Surviving Provisions. The provisions of Sections 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (except for subsections 10.2 and 10.3), 11, 12, 13, and 14 shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

10.5 Remedies. The termination of this Agreement shall not limit the Lender's rights and remedies with respect to any event of default that occurred prior to termination.

10.6 Notice. Any notice of termination shall be delivered to the Borrower's address as set forth in Section 12.

10.7 Effect of Termination. Upon termination of this Agreement, the security interests granted by the Borrower to the Lender shall be released, except to the extent necessary to secure any amounts owed by the Borrower to the Lender that have not been paid in full.
