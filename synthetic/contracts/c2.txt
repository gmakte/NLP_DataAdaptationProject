8. Interest and Fees
8.1 Interest: The Borrower shall pay interest on the Loan at a rate of 12% per annum, calculated on the daily balance of the Loan and compounded monthly.

  8.2 Fees: The Borrower shall pay all fees and expenses incurred by the Lender in connection with the Loan, including but not limited to:

    8.2.1 Origination fee in the amount of 2% of the Loan principal.

    8.2.2 Documentation fee in the amount of $1,500.

    8.2.3 Processing fee in the amount of $500.

  8.3 Late Fees: If the Borrower fails to make any payment when due, the Borrower shall pay a late fee in the amount of 5% of the overdue amount, or the maximum amount allowed by law, whichever is less.

  8.4 Other Charges: The Borrower shall pay any other charges, fees, or expenses incurred by the Lender in connection with the Loan, including but not limited to attorney's fees, court costs, and expenses incurred in the collection of any amounts due under this Agreement.

  8.5 Payment of Fees and Charges: The Borrower shall pay all fees and charges described in this Section 8 concurrently with the disbursement of the Loan. If the Loan is not disbursed, the Borrower shall pay all fees and charges described in this Section 8 upon the request of the Lender.

  8.6 Interest on Late Payments: If any amount due under this Agreement is not paid when due, the Borrower shall pay interest on such overdue amount at the rate of 12% per annum, calculated on a daily basis.

  8.7 Application of Payments: Payments made by the Borrower under this Agreement shall first be applied to any late fees, interest, and other charges due under this Agreement, and then to the principal of the Loan and any accrued and unpaid interest.
