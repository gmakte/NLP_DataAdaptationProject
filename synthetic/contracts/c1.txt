This Agreement is entered into as of January 1, 2023, by and between Goldman Sachs Bank, USA, a national banking association organized and existing under the laws of the United States of America, with its principal place of business at One New York Plaza, New York, NY 10004, USA (hereinafter referred to as "Lender"), and Acme Inc., a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the United States of America, with its principal place of business at 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA 12345 (hereinafter referred to as "Borrower"). 6. Interest and Fees

6.1 Interest Rate
The interest rate on the Loan shall be 5% per annum, calculated on the daily balance outstanding.

6.2 Fees
The Borrower shall pay all fees and expenses incurred by the Lender in connection with the Loan, including but not limited to, commitment fees, processing fees, and legal fees.

6.3 Payment Terms
The Borrower shall make payments on the Loan on a monthly basis, commencing on the first day of the month following the Disbursement Date and continuing on the first day of each month thereafter, until the Loan is fully repaid.

7. Default and Remedies

7.1 Default
A Default under this Agreement shall occur if any of the following events occur:

(a) The Borrower fails to make any payment when due;
(b) The Borrower breaches any of its representations and warranties contained in this Agreement;
(c) The Borrower becomes insolvent or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors;
(d) The Borrower files a petition under any bankruptcy or insolvency laws, or has a petition filed against it under any bankruptcy or insolvency laws, and the petition is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;
(e) The Borrower disposes of, or attempts to dispose of, any of its assets outside the ordinary course of business; or
(f) The Borrower fails to perform any other material obligation under this Agreement.

7.2 Remedies
In the event of a Default, Goldman Sachs Bank, USA shall have the right to declare all amounts outstanding under this Agreement immediately due and payable, and to exercise any and all rights and remedies available to it under applicable law, including the right to foreclose on any security provided for the Loan, and to seize and sell the assets of both Acme Inc., USA and Acme Corp., USA to recover the Loan amount.

7.3 Termination
Upon the occurrence of a Default, this Agreement shall terminate, except that the provisions relating to representations and warranties, Default, remedies, security, governing law, and signatures shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

(i) As security for the payment and performance of the Borrower's obligations under this Agreement and the Loan, the Borrower hereby grants to the Lender a first priority lien on all of the Borrower's assets, present and future, whether now owned or hereafter acquired, wherever located, including, without limitation, all of the Borrower's accounts, chattel paper, documents, instruments, equipment, fixtures, vehicles, inventory, intellectual property, and all other personal property, and all of the Borrower's right, title, and interest in and to the same, including, without limitation, all of the Borrower's accounts receivable, contract rights, and other intangible property.

(ii) The Borrower hereby grants to the Lender a second priority lien on all of the assets of Acme Corp., USA, present and future, whether now owned or hereafter acquired, wherever located, including, without limitation, all of Acme Corp., USA's accounts, chattel paper, documents, instruments, equipment, fixtures, vehicles, inventory, intellectual property, and all other personal property, and all of Acme Corp., USA's right, title, and interest in and to the same, including, without limitation, all of Acme Corp., USA's accounts receivable, contract rights, and other intangible property.

(iv) The Borrower and Acme Corp., USA agree to execute and deliver such additional documents and instruments, and to take such further action as the Lender may reasonably request, in order to further secure the Loan and to evidence the liens granted hereunder.

(v) The liens granted hereunder shall be subordinate only to the liens of the Borrower's and Acme Corp., USA's respective creditors in the ordinary course of business, and the liens granted hereunder shall not be affected by any subsequent consolidation, merger, reorganization, or sale of the Borrower or Acme Corp., USA, or any of their respective subsidiaries.

(vi) The Borrower and Acme Corp., USA shall keep the Lender fully and currently informed of the location and condition of the assets securing the Loan, and shall promptly notify the Lender of any change in the location or condition of such assets.

(vii) In the event of any default under this Agreement or the Loan, the Borrower and Acme Corp., USA shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including, without limitation, attorneys' fees and expenses, incurred by the Lender in connection with the enforcement of the liens granted hereunder.
