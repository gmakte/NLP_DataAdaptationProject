    LOAN AGREEMENT

    January 14, 2021

    BETWEEN:

    XYZ Bank (hereinafter referred to as "Lender"), a duly incorporated bank
    organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its
    principal place of business at 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA 12345;

    AND:

    John Doe (hereinafter referred to as "Borrower"), a natural person,
    residing at 456 Park Avenue, Anytown, USA 56789.

    WHEREAS, Lender desires to extend credit to Borrower in the amount of
    One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000); and

    NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions
    contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

    1. LOAN TERMS

    a. Lender shall extend to Borrower a loan in the principal amount of
       One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000), with interest at the rate of
       8% per annum, compounded semi-annually.

    b. The loan shall be due and payable in full on January 14, 2023.

    c. Borrower may prepay the loan in whole or in part at any time without
       penalty.

    2. INTEREST AND FEES

    a. Borrower agrees to pay Lender interest on the outstanding principal
       balance of the loan as set forth in Section 1(a).

    b. Borrower shall pay a one-time origination fee equal to 2% of the
       principal amount of the loan, due upon disbursement of the loan.

    3. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

    a. In the event Borrower fails to make any payment when due, the entire
       outstanding balance of the loan, including accrued and unpaid interest,
       shall become immediately due and payable.

    b. Lender may, without limiting any other rights or remedies available to
       it, declare all or any part of the outstanding loan balance and any
       accrued but unpaid interest to be immediately due and payable, exercise
       any rights of setoff, repossession, or foreclosure to which it may be
       entitled, and take any other action to protect its interests.

    4. SECURITY

    This loan shall be secured by a first priority lien on all of Borrower's
    assets, as described in the Security Agreement executed by the parties
    concurrently with this Agreement.

    5. MISCELLANEOUS

    a. This Agreement may not be amended or modified except by a written
       instrument executed by both parties.

    b. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
       the laws of the State of Delaware.

    c. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties
       concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior
       negotiations, understandings, and agreements between the parties,
       whether written or oral.

    IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the
    date first above written.
